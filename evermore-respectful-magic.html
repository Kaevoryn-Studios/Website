RESPECTFUL MAGIC

Two ways to show how technology can help tell a meaningful story—while respecting the real person behind the memory.

DEMO 01

A Photograph Comes to Life

Use gentle movement and cinematic lighting to animate an authorized photo, without changing who the person was.

Show the simple process
  1. Keep the original photo unchanged.
  2. Try subtle camera movement, soft light, or background motion.
  3. Review the face and details; undo anything distorted or disrespectful.
  4. Label the result: “AI-animated image — generated motion, not original video.”
☼

DEMO 02

A Memory Becomes a Movie

Turn authorized video clips into a tribute with thoughtful editing, captions, music, and visual effects.

Show the simple process
  1. Keep a copy of the original footage and confirm permission.
  2. Arrange a few meaningful clips with a title and gentle ending.
  3. Use music and effects you are allowed to use; keep real speech intact.
  4. Credit the source and disclose any AI-generated scenery or effects.

Our promise of respect

Robin Williams and Walt Disney are meaningful examples of creative legacies. If we feature them, we should use appropriately authorized archival material and avoid invented dialogue or imitations presented as real.

Read our full respect checklist
  • Preserve the original media; never overwrite it.
  • Follow permission and rights attached to photos, recordings, and clips.
  • Do not invent quotes, endorsements, or private conversations.
  • Clearly disclose AI animation and synthetic additions.
  • Let families decide whether to participate, share, or remove a tribute.
  • Always offer a non-AI slideshow or original-video version.

Technology can help preserve and celebrate memories; it cannot bring someone back.

Ready to plan your own film?

Use the storyboard to arrange the story first. You can make a simple photo slideshow today; automatic AI animation is a future feature, not something Evermore currently does.
