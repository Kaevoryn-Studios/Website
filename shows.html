Night Spectacular — Master Run of Show ↗

This is the expanded creative run sheet for the first full-length draft. The running times below are provisional planning targets, not soundtrack-locked timecode. Once the original music is selected, every cue must be re-timed against the actual audio.

PREVIEW ACT 1 →PREVIEW ACT 3 →PREVIEW FINALE →
KNS-00 · PRE-SHOW · BEFORE 00:00

Ready the room

Picture: Hold a calm midnight-blue star field; keep essential paths and exits visible in any real venue.

Audio: Optional low-level ambience only after the operator confirms the playback route.

Operator check: Confirm the correct show file, sound level, visual output, accessibility arrangements, weather/site conditions, and a tested pause/stop method.

GO: The show lead confirms readiness. If not ready, hold—never improvise physical effects.

ACT I · 00:00–01:30 TARGET

The Stillness — the first spark

Picture: Begin nearly dark, reveal distant stars, then bring up one small cyan point. Let the audience discover it.

Sound: Soft atmosphere, a low warm tone, then one restrained pulse. No sudden blast.

Narration draft: “Every impossible thing begins as a thought. Every great journey begins with a spark.”

Transition: The spark sends its first ring across the horizon.

ACT II · 01:30–03:00 TARGET

The Awakening — the Core opens

Picture: Cyan rings form around the Core. Violet energy gathers in layers; a portal outline gradually appears.

Sound: Introduce the signature motif and a measured rhythmic pulse. Build in stages rather than all at once.

Narration draft: “Look closer. The world is not finished. There are doors where we once saw walls.”

Transition: A light trail leads through the opening portal.

ACT III · 03:00–06:00 TARGET

Wonder — worlds beyond the screen

Picture: Travel through three distinct visual chapters: celestial star paths, fantastical architecture, and a futuristic energy horizon. Give each world a clear silhouette and breathing room.

Sound: Add melody, percussion, and orchestral-synth layers in waves. Each world gets its own musical color while retaining the Core motif.

Narration draft: “Imagine what has never been seen. Shape what has never been made. There is more beyond the horizon than we know.”

Transition: The three worlds' light trails converge into a single golden spark.

ACT IV · 06:00–07:30 TARGET

The Heart — the reason we dream

Picture: Motion softens. A warm golden center glows against the cyan-violet night; keep the image uncluttered and still.

Sound: Pull back to the central melody. Allow a real pause; do not fill every second with sound.

Narration draft: “Kaevoryn isn't just a place. It's the magic in your heart. The next impossible thing begins with you.”

Transition: The golden spark returns to the Core and begins to brighten.

ACT V · 07:30–09:30 TARGET

Create the Impossible — the finale

Picture: Reunite the stars, rings, portal, three world motifs, and golden spark. Build to a broad cyan-violet-gold horizon, then resolve to a clean Kaevoryn signature frame.

Sound: Bring back the main theme at its fullest planned intensity, then land on a clear final chord with a natural decay.

Final narration draft: “Dream the impossible. Envision what could be. And create it anyway.”

Final title: “KAEVORYN — CREATE THE IMPOSSIBLE.” Hold, then fade gently to the closing image.

POST-SHOW · AFTER THE FINAL HOLD

Leave the dream glowing

Allow the final image and music to resolve before returning to the normal environment. For a website preview, provide a clear replay control. For a future venue production, audience movement, house lighting, exits, and announcements must be planned with the venue team.

HOLD / ABORT RULE

Safety takes priority over spectacle

If a technical fault, unsafe condition, weather concern, or audience-care issue arises, the show lead must be able to pause or stop safely. Lasers, pyrotechnics, drones, water, and other physical effects require qualified operators, site-specific risk assessment, approvals, and tested emergency procedures. This creative run sheet is not an operating manual.

🏰 A WORLD TO DISCOVER

Kaevoryn Citadel

A landmark in a fantasy universe: mysterious halls, hidden discoveries, and places that invite dreamers to look beyond the obvious. It could eventually connect stories and interactive experiences.

Planned feeling: wonder, exploration, and ancient mystery.

WORLD CONCEPT — STORY TREATMENT ADDED

The Citadel's five discoveries

01 · THE ARRIVAL

The Gate That Remembers

At dusk, a sealed gate lights up with tiny constellations. It responds to the visitor's approach with a low, welcoming glow—never a jump scare.

Sound identity: distant wind, a single glassy bell, and a three-note original Citadel motif.

02 · THE HALL

Architecture in Motion

Star maps drift across the ceiling while luminous lines trace the shape of impossible towers. The building feels ancient, but the magic is alive.

Sound identity: soft strings, stone-like resonance, and measured footsteps in a wide acoustic space.

03 · THE LIBRARY

Pages of Unwritten Worlds

Floating pages open into miniature moving landscapes. Each one suggests a story without locking the audience into a single path.

Sound identity: delicate page turns, airy chimes, and a curious plucked melody.

04 · THE HEART

The Lantern Chamber

A quiet central chamber holds a suspended lantern. Its light grows when the visitor discovers a hidden symbol, rewarding attention rather than speed.

Sound identity: warm sustained tones, a gentle pulse, and a deliberate pocket of silence.

05 · THE REVEAL

The Citadel Is a Compass

The towers align into a constellation pointing toward a new horizon. The place is not a fortress keeping dreams in; it is a guide helping them find their way out.

Sound identity: the original Citadel motif returns with fuller strings and a bright final bell, distinct from Night Spectacular's large-scale finale.

Production rule: Citadel is an exploratory fantasy journey, not a nighttime fireworks show. Its magic comes from noticing, discovery, spatial sound, hidden light, and small emotional payoffs. This is a written treatment; no physical attraction or recorded score is complete yet.

INTERACTIVE WEBSITE EXPERIENCE — FIRST VERSION

The welcome sequence

01 · THE QUIET INVITATION

A world at rest

The page begins calm and readable. A few distant particles drift around a subtle central glow. No sound starts automatically, and the visitor can continue immediately.

Audio: silence by default; optional soft atmospheric tone after a clear user action.

02 · THE PRESENCE

Something notices you

A gentle cyan pulse appears, then a second light answers from the violet edge of the screen. The scene suggests a presence without depicting a face or startling the visitor.

Audio: two original chimes with generous space between them.

03 · THE CORE

The Core is awakening

Rings and particles gather around the Core. The words “PRESENCE DETECTED” and “WELCOME, DREAMER” can appear as part of the sequence, with enough contrast and reading time.

Enter, or continue

The glow opens outward and presents a clear next step. Skip, reduced-motion, and keyboard-friendly access remain available; the welcome never blocks the site's main content.

Audio: a brief warm resolution only if sound was enabled.

Production rule: this is the site's front door, not a separate long-form show. Keep it short, optional, quiet by default, and respectful of the visitor's control.

👁 SUPERNATURAL HORROR

Ghost of Kickapoo

A haunting mystery built around eerie legends, unsettling clues, and the sense that something from the past has never truly left.

STORY TREATMENT ADDED — NOT A FINISHED SHOW

Four beats of a slow-burn haunting

01 · THE RUMOR

A Light Across the Water

A distant light appears where no building should stand. Locals disagree about what it means, and the story begins with unanswered questions rather than a visible ghost.

02 · THE TRACE

Evidence in the Static

A recording catches a sound that was not heard in the moment: three soft knocks followed by a fragment of a melody. The clue invites investigation; it does not prove a supernatural cause.

03 · THE MEMORY

The Name Left Behind

Old notes reveal a person whose story was forgotten. The haunting becomes emotionally grounded in a life and a choice, not simply in frightening imagery.

04 · THE ANSWER

Let the Night Speak

The final encounter reveals a meaningful truth but leaves one small mystery unresolved. The ending is eerie and haunting, not a loud jump-scare montage.

Sound and visual identity: restrained field ambience, distant wood creaks, a recurring three-knock motif, long shadows, and carefully controlled silence. No automatic loud scares in any future interactive version. This is an original fictional treatment; no location-specific claims are being presented as fact.

🕯 HAUNTED MYSTERY

Haunted Kickapoo Mansion

A haunted setting full of sealed rooms, hidden histories, and strange discoveries, with room to grow into a story, game, or immersive experience.

STORY TREATMENT ADDED — NOT A FINISHED SHOW

Four rooms, four pieces of the puzzle

01 · THE FOYER

The Clock That Runs Backward

A stopped clock moves only when a visitor finds a detail out of place. Its hands point toward a room the floor plan does not show.

02 · THE MUSIC ROOM

The Last Unfinished Measure

An old instrument plays a few notes when a hidden drawer opens. The melody is a clue, and a future original score could echo it in later scenes.

03 · THE SEALED ROOM

Dust Without Footprints

Objects have been carefully preserved, but one space is strangely untouched. The visitor assembles the history from letters, portraits, and environmental details.

04 · THE ATTIC

The House Remembers

The clues connect into a reveal about why the room was sealed. The finale rewards curiosity and observation rather than requiring a chase or a fight.

Sound and visual identity: intimate creaks, clockwork ticks, a slightly detuned music-box phrase, candlelike practical glows, and layered environmental storytelling. Unlike Ghost of Kickapoo's outdoor folklore mystery, the Mansion is a close-up puzzle journey through a single building.

🔮 FANTASY · OPTICAL WONDER

Castle of Illusion

A fantastical castle where appearances may deceive and every passage might reveal a new wonder, with room for visual illusions, puzzles, and an unfolding story.

STORY TREATMENT ADDED — NOT A FINISHED SHOW

Four illusions that change the story

01 · THE THRESHOLD

The Door in the Painting

A painted doorway seems ordinary until its shadows move in the wrong direction. The visitor discovers that the picture is a map, not a door.

02 · THE GALLERY

Portraits That Look Away

Portraits appear to watch the room, but their gazes align into a clue when viewed from a particular spot. The effect is playful and mysterious, not threatening.

03 · THE IMPOSSIBLE STAIR

Up, Down, and Around

Mirrors, forced perspective, and shifting light make a short corridor feel endless. A repeated symbol helps the visitor understand the trick and solve the route.

04 · THE TRUE ROOM

Nothing Was What It Seemed

The illusions resolve into one clear pattern: the castle has been protecting a hidden light. The finale transforms confusion into clarity and gives the visitor a satisfying reveal.

Sound and visual identity: playful orchestral pizzicato, shimmering glass tones, mirrored compositions, impossible geometry, and visual reveals timed to musical accents. Unlike Citadel's ancient exploratory atmosphere, Castle of Illusion is built around perception, clever puzzles, and delightful reversals. Keep it original and avoid copying any existing attraction or franchise.

🎼 ORIGINAL MUSIC BRIEFING · NEXT SHOWS

Five worlds. Five different musical identities.

These copy-ready prompts are for the other show concepts. Generate each as its own instrumental cue or theme; do not reuse the Night Spectacular score for every world. Durations are targets only, and the music may need editing to fit the final scenes.

CITADEL · EXPLORATION

Kaevoryn Citadel — The Gate Remembers

Target: 2–3 minutes, instrumental fantasy exploration theme.

Prompt: Compose an original cinematic fantasy instrumental for exploring an ancient, luminous citadel at dusk. Begin with distant wind, a single glassy bell, and a recognizable three-note motif. Gradually introduce warm strings, restrained low percussion, delicate harp or plucked textures, and spacious reverberation. Move through mystery, discovery, a quiet lantern-chamber emotional moment, then a hopeful constellation reveal. Elegant, ancient, curious, magical, and welcoming—not a battle march, not a giant fireworks finale. No vocals, no recognizable existing melodies, no imitation of a named artist or franchise.

WELCOME SPECTACLE · FIRST CONTACT

The Core Is Awakening

Target: 30–60 seconds, short optional website welcome cue.

Prompt: Create a brief original ambient-cinematic welcome cue for a futuristic magical world. Start nearly silent with soft air and a distant cyan-like shimmer, add two gentle answering chimes, then a memorable three-note signature as light gathers into a radiant Core. Resolve warmly and quickly, leaving room for the words “PRESENCE DETECTED” and “WELCOME, DREAMER.” Keep dynamics gentle, no sudden impacts, no vocals, no horror stingers, and no imitation of existing franchise music.

GHOST OF KICKAPOO · FOLKLORE

A Light Across the Water

Target: 2–3 minutes, slow-burn supernatural mystery cue.

Prompt: Compose an original restrained supernatural mystery score for a fictional night landscape. Use distant wind, soft bowed textures, sparse low piano, subtle environmental creaks, and a recurring three-knock rhythm separated by long silence. Let a fragile melody gradually reveal an emotional memory, then end with one unresolved but quiet note. Eerie and atmospheric rather than loud or aggressive; no jump-scare hits, no vocals, no copied melodies, and no imitation of any named horror film or composer.

HAUNTED MANSION · PUZZLE MYSTERY

The Last Unfinished Measure

Target: 2–3 minutes, intimate haunted-house exploration cue.

Prompt: Create an original intimate mystery instrumental for exploring a forgotten mansion. Build a musical motif from a slightly detuned music-box phrase, quiet clockwork ticks, muted piano, low strings, and occasional room-tone creaks. Each section should feel like a new clue: foyer, music room, sealed room, attic. Grow more emotional as the clues connect, then resolve with a delicate, bittersweet final phrase. Avoid loud stingers, chase music, vocals, and recognizable melodies or franchise imitation.

CASTLE OF ILLUSION · PLAYFUL WONDER

The Door in the Painting

Target: 2–3 minutes, clever fantasy puzzle theme.

Prompt: Compose an original playful cinematic fantasy instrumental for a castle where paintings, mirrors, and impossible staircases reveal hidden patterns. Use nimble pizzicato strings, celesta or glassy mallet tones, light woodwinds, shifting rhythmic accents, and brief musical misdirections that resolve into satisfying phrases. Make it curious, elegant, surprising, and joyful rather than spooky or grandiose. Build toward a bright revelation, then finish cleanly. No vocals, no existing theme references, and no imitation of a named artist or franchise.

Music workflow: generate the themes separately, save the original exports, label them with the show and cue name, and keep the untouched original files. After the music is chosen, we can trim and time the cues against each show's story beats.
