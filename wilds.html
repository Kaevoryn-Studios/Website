THE EXPEDITION BEGINS

Six territories. One untamed world.

From the first river crossing to the heart of a lost empire, every territory reveals another part of the Wilds.

🌴

Jungle Outpost

Your base camp at the edge of the unknown. Supply depots, rope bridges, expedition guides, and trails into the green.

🦖

Valley of Giants

A vast prehistoric valley where enormous creatures roam through fern forests, cliffs, and mist-covered lakes.

🏛️

The Sunken Empire

Ancient stone avenues, overgrown plazas, colossal statues, and a royal temple hidden beneath the canopy.

🌋

Ember Mountain

A rugged volcanic frontier of black rock, steaming vents, glowing caverns, and a river that winds toward the crater.

💧

Emerald Falls

A spectacular waterfall basin with hidden caves, hanging gardens, river rapids, and quiet pools beneath the mist.

🧭

The Forbidden Pass

A high-risk expedition route through ravines, rope bridges, ancient watchtowers, and the final sealed gateway.
