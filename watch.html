HOW VIEWING WILL WORK

Simple to find. Easy to watch.

  1. Choose a show, film, or special.
  2. Open its page to see the description and available videos.
  3. Press play on a published trailer or episode when one is available.

For the first version, videos can be embedded from a supported video host. That avoids the cost and complexity of operating our own streaming servers. We will add actual playback links only when the videos are ready and hosted.
