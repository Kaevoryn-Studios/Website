BUILT WITH CARE

Memories belong to the people who entrust them to us.

Evermore is planned for individuals and families. The first version will focus on private collections and personal tributes before advanced AI experiences are considered.

Private by defaultOnly the owner can access a memory unless they choose to share it.
Honest about AIGenerated animation, voices, or conversations will never be presented as the actual person returning.
Consent mattersAny likeness or voice recreation must follow appropriate permission and safeguards.
Control stays with youFuture memory tools should make it possible to export or delete your saved materials.

Start with the memories. Grow the magic carefully.

Our first goal is a simple, secure Memory Vault. Photo animation, authorized voice experiences, and immersive digital remembrance can follow in later stages—once privacy, cost, and technology are ready.

A CAREFUL JOURNEY

One meaningful step at a time.

STAGE 01

Keep the memories

Plan the private vault, memory collections, captions, dates, and export or delete controls.

STAGE 02

Tell the story

Shape personal tributes and family timelines, with sharing controlled by the owner.

STAGE 03

Bring moments to life

Explore optional photo animation and clearly labeled generated experiences only after consent, privacy, and costs are addressed.
