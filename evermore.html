EVERMORE

KAEVORYN · FOREVER IN OUR HEARTS

A thoughtful place to preserve a loved one’s stories, revisit the moments that shaped us, and keep the love behind those memories close.

Love remembered. Moments treasured. Stories that live on.

Early concept · In development
This page introduces the vision. Private photo uploads, cloud memory storage, and AI animations are not available here yet. Please don’t upload personal photographs or recordings to this concept page.
♡

The Memory Vault

A private collection for photographs, personal stories, voice recordings, favorite sayings, and the little details that make a person unforgettable.

  • Organize by person or occasion
  • Add dates, captions, and stories
  • Keep memories private by default
✧

Special Moments

Create a remembrance for a birthday, anniversary, holiday, family milestone, or a quiet day when you simply miss someone.

  • Tributes and memory timelines
  • Letters and personal farewells
  • Optional sharing with trusted family
☼

Living Memories

Over time, explore gentle photo animation, meaningful music, and short memory films made from materials you have permission to use.

  • Respectful photo-to-video tools
  • Original recordings preserved
  • Generated content clearly labeled

BUILT WITH CARE

Memories belong to the people who entrust them to us.

Evermore is planned for individuals and families. The first version will focus on private collections and personal tributes before advanced AI experiences are considered.

Private by defaultOnly the owner can access a memory unless they choose to share it.
Honest about AIGenerated animation, voices, or conversations will never be presented as the actual person returning.
Consent mattersAny likeness or voice recreation must follow appropriate permission and safeguards.
Control stays with youFuture memory tools should make it possible to export or delete your saved materials.

Start with the memories. Grow the magic carefully.

Our first goal is a simple, secure Memory Vault. Photo animation, authorized voice experiences, and immersive digital remembrance can follow in later stages—once privacy, cost, and technology are ready.
