The Kaevoryn Dream Parade

An animated parade designed for phones and computers, bringing together original characters, cinematic music, magical lighting, and a finale worthy of the Kaevoryn universe.

🐉

Magical Floats & Living Characters

Fantasy-inspired floats, dragons, funny personalities, mysterious presences, and rare golden appearances.
