How to turn the plan into a real video

1. Gather and organize

  1. Choose photos and clips you own or have permission to use.
  2. Make a copy of the originals and arrange selected files in story order.
  3. Ask family for accurate names, dates, captions, and stories.

2. Assemble the timeline

  1. Open a trusted video editor and start a new project.
  2. Import media and place it in the planned order.
  3. Use simple cuts or gentle transitions; keep faces visible and text readable.
  4. Add captions and a title card, then check the pacing.

3. Add sound and review

  1. Use music you have permission to play or share.
  2. Keep music below any spoken words.
  3. Ask a trusted person to review spelling, names, and sensitive moments.
  4. Preview the entire film with sound before export.

4. Export and prepare

  1. Export a widely compatible video file, such as MP4 if available.
  2. Test it on the exact TV, laptop, or event system.
  3. Keep an offline backup and a non-AI version.
  4. Confirm who may keep, view, or share the final file.

Optional future AI animation

Evermore does not create photo animations yet. If you use another service, check consent, privacy, retention, and deletion terms first. Label generated motion clearly and preserve the original photograph. AI animation is not recovered footage of the person.

For projection or VR

Projection needs a suitable projector, surface, lighting, and testing. A VR version is a future concept and would need compatible hardware, comfort controls, an easy exit, consent safeguards, and a screen-based alternative.
