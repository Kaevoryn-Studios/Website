PROPOSED CUE SHEET

A journey with a beginning, a build, and a payoff

These are planning durations for a short first prototype, not a final venue running time. Music, lighting, projection, and any future physical effects would be synchronized to a shared timecode during professional production.

00:00–00:20

01 · The World Holds Its Breath

Look: Near-black sky, distant stars, a faint horizon, one tiny cyan pulse.

Sound: Optional low atmospheric tone and a slow heartbeat-like original percussion cue.

Feeling: Anticipation. Let the audience notice the first spark before the world changes.

00:20–00:45

02 · The Core Awakens

Look: Concentric rings, energy threads, particles gathering into a radiant central Core.

Show language: The light pulses grow in scale with the music; each layer feels earned rather than random.

Feeling: Discovery—the Kaevoryn symbol becomes a living presence.

00:45–01:15

03 · The Invitation

Look: The Core releases a portal; luminous ribbons sweep outward and a new world appears beyond it.

Future stage translation: Projection mapping or large-format surfaces could carry the transformation, with lighting adding depth around the audience.

Feeling: The audience is invited into the story, not merely watching from outside.

01:15–01:50

04 · Worlds Beyond Imagination

Look: A sequence of original environments—cosmic ocean, floating crystal architecture, a star-forged forest—each with its own color and motion language.

Sound: An original melody develops new orchestral layers and rhythmic energy.

Feeling: Scale and wonder, with clear visual transitions that the audience can follow.

01:50–02:15

05 · The Magic in Your Heart

Look: The spectacle suddenly breathes. The Core becomes warm, gentle, and intimate; a field of light gathers like a constellation.

Sound: A brief musical release creates room for the emotional message.

Feeling: The reveal that imagination belongs to every dreamer.

02:15–02:45

06 · Create the Impossible

Look: Cyan, violet, and gold energy converges. A radial burst expands into a brilliant sky-wide constellation, then resolves into the Kaevoryn mark and words.

Final words: CREATE THE IMPOSSIBLE.

Feeling: A decisive, uplifting finish—followed by a final beat of stillness so the moment can land.

How the versions fit together: the six short cue-sheet beats are production moments, not six separate acts. They are intended to sit inside the larger five-act Night Spectacular story. The browser preview is a separate, approximately 40-second visual prototype; this 2:45 cue sheet is a short-form planning draft; the proposed full show is a longer, not-yet-produced version. Their timings are not interchangeable.

ORIGINAL SCORE DEVELOPMENT

A musical identity only Kaevoryn could own

The score should grow from a small, memorable three-note Core motif into a full, uplifting finale. These are creative direction notes for a future original composition—not a finished recording or licensed track.

MOTIF A

The Core · three-note signature

Begin with spacious, bell-like tones and a gentle low pulse. The motif should be simple enough to recognize when it returns at the finale.

MOTIF B

The Invitation · rising movement

Add airy textures, soft strings, and a rising rhythmic pattern as the portal opens. Let each new world introduce a fresh color while keeping the Core motif present.

MOTIF C

The Heart · space and warmth

Pull back the percussion and leave room for a tender melodic statement. The contrast makes the final build feel earned instead of constantly loud.

FINALE

One theme, full power

Bring back the Core motif with fuller orchestration, strong rhythmic accents, and a clear final hit. Leave a short silence after “CREATE THE IMPOSSIBLE” so the message can land.

FULL SHOW · FIVE-ACT NARRATION DRAFT

The complete story of First Light

This is the long-form narration pass for the proposed 9-minute-30-second version. It follows the five-act master run sheet. These are creative timing windows, not locked playback timecode; the recorded performance and final music must determine exact cue points.

ACT I
00:00–01:30

The Stillness — the first spark

Opening: Begin in silence. Let the first star appear before the voice enters.

Voice draft: “Before every great journey, there is a moment when the world grows quiet. Before every invention, every story, every impossible dream… there is a thought. Small enough to fit inside a heart. Powerful enough to change what comes next. And somewhere, in the stillness, a spark is waiting.”

Delivery: Gentle, close, and curious. Leave open space between sentences; do not narrate over the first visual reveal.

Exit cue: On “a spark is waiting,” the cyan point brightens and sends the first ring across the horizon.

ACT II
01:30–03:00

The Awakening — the Core opens

Voice draft: “Look closer. The world is not finished. What seems impossible may only be something we have not discovered yet. A wall can become a doorway. A question can become a journey. A single spark can become a thousand paths. The Core is awakening… and it is inviting us to imagine what lies beyond.”

Delivery: Add quiet confidence as the music grows. Pause after “The Core is awakening” so the central visual has room to bloom.

Exit cue: The portal completes its outline; a single light trail leads into Act III.

ACT III
03:00–06:00

Wonder — worlds beyond the screen

Voice draft: “Imagine a sea made of stars. A citadel shaped by dreams. A horizon where tomorrow's ideas dance with the magic of the unknown. Every world begins with a question: what if? What if we looked again? What if we built something new? What if the boundaries we see are only the beginning? Follow the light. Let curiosity lead. There are worlds beyond the horizon… and the next one begins with you.”

Delivery: Bright, inviting, and full of discovery. Let the three world chapters carry stretches of music without narration; each world needs its own visual breathing room.

Exit cue: Distinct trails from the worlds converge into one warm golden spark.

ACT IV
06:00–07:30

The Heart — the reason we dream

Voice draft: “And now, let the worlds grow quiet. For all the places we can travel, all the things we can make, and all the dreams still waiting to be born, the most important spark is closer than it seems. Kaevoryn isn't just a place. It's the magic in your heart. The next impossible thing begins with you.”

Delivery: Warm, sincere, and intimate. Lower the score beneath the words and leave a meaningful pause after “your heart.”

Exit cue: The golden spark returns to the Core. Hold the image briefly before the final build.

ACT V
07:30–09:30

Create the Impossible — the finale

Voice draft: “Carry the wonder with you. Dream beyond what you know. Envision what has never been. Take that first small spark, and give it a world to become. The future is not only something we wait for. It is something we imagine… and create. Kaevoryn. Create the Impossible.”

Delivery: Build in strength without shouting. Place “Kaevoryn” just before the final title reveal, then let “Create the Impossible” land with the music's final resolved hit.

Final hold: Display “KAEVORYN — CREATE THE IMPOSSIBLE.” Let the chord decay and preserve a short moment of stillness before the safe fade.

Script status: original text draft only. It has not been voiced, timed against the music archive, or recorded. Keep the performance original, caption every spoken line, and preserve clear gaps where the visuals should speak for themselves.

READINESS GATES

What must be true before we move forward

WEB PREVIEW

Experience works for everyone

Test on mobile and desktop; keep sound off by default; confirm pause, replay, skip, progress, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion behavior.

CREATIVE RIGHTS

Everything is original or cleared

Use original story, visual design, music, and branding, or properly licensed assets. Keep a record of permissions for any third-party material.

PRODUCTION DESIGN

Every cue has an owner

For a physical show, assign each time-coded cue to a qualified operator, define dependencies and fallback states, and rehearse transitions before audience testing.

PUBLIC PERFORMANCE

Safety is part of the design

Confirm venue approval, permits, accessibility, weather and power contingencies, audience sightlines, emergency stop procedures, and qualified operators before any live effects.

Gate rule: do not treat a milestone as complete just because the page exists. Test it, document the result, and fix issues before advancing.

How this becomes a real show

First we finish and test the on-screen preview. Next we develop original music and a time-coded show file. A physical production would then require a suitable site, specialist designers and operators, budget, rights-cleared content, permits, accessibility planning, and safety review. Lasers, pyrotechnics, water effects, haze, drones, or other high-risk effects would only be considered with appropriately qualified professionals and venue approval.

Our promise for now: build the creative vision honestly, one achievable milestone at a time. This page is a concept blueprint; it does not mean a physical show has been booked, funded, or built.
