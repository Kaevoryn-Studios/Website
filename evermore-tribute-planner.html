A gentle event checklist

Before the day

  • Ask what the person or family would be comfortable with.
  • Choose the photos, stories, song, and length.
  • Confirm permissions and who can access the files.

At the event

  • Let the host introduce the tribute if they wish.
  • Keep a trusted person ready to start and stop playback.
  • Offer a quiet space or a way to step out.

After the moment

  • Ask before sharing photos or recordings of attendees.
  • Store or delete the finished file according to the family's wishes.
  • Remember that no one has to participate in the same way.

About AI

AI animation or a digital character is a generated representation, not the actual person returning. Use appropriate permission, label generated media, and avoid invented dialogue presented as real words.
